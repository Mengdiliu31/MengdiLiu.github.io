Ipsos · Internship project

Making sense of the personal-care market

Consolidated 89 campaign records across 49 brands into a monthly competitive tracker, comparing campaign timing, partnerships, buzz, and engagement across three personal-care categories.

ExcelSocial listeningCompetitive analysis
Read the project details

Objective: Organize campaign and consumer-feedback data to support brand and campaign evaluation.

My approach: Applied Boolean keyword filters, data cleaning rules, pivot tables, and engagement metrics using Excel and Ipsos’ analytics platform.

  • Structured positive and negative feedback into themes such as efficacy, skin feel, irritation, fragrance, pricing, and customer service.
  • Reviewed six TikTok and Xiaohongshu brand-platform datasets and selected 40–50 relevant keywords per dataset for competitive word clouds.

Output: A monthly competitive tracker and structured consumer-insight inputs.

Reference: Ipsos internship, August 2026–present. This case study summarizes the work; internal datasets and company materials are not included.
